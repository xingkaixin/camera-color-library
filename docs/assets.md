# 示例素材

这两张照片使用内置 imagegen 生成，作为 App 离线体验素材，不是实际拍摄的旅行记录。用户图片不参与生成。素材已复制进工程，不依赖工作区之外的路径。

- 咖啡馆：`ColorLibrary/Resources/Assets.xcassets/Cafe.imageset/cafe.png`
- 海岸：`ColorLibrary/Resources/Assets.xcassets/Coast.imageset/coast.png`

示例卡片中的色带为编辑预览；点按示例后会运行与用户照片相同的提取算法。

## 最终提示词

### Cafe

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bundled sample photograph for an iOS personal color palette library. Generate a vertical 3:4 editorial interior photograph of a quiet Mediterranean coffee corner: warm terracotta plaster wall, a small ivory stone table, olive green wooden chair, ceramic espresso cup, one dried branch, diagonal warm late-afternoon sunlight and crisp architectural shadows. Artful close framing with strong blocks of earth colors, warm off-white, rust, olive, cocoa. Analog film texture, believable materials, understated independent design magazine art direction. No people, no typography, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. Single full-bleed photograph.

### Coast

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bundled sample photograph for an iOS personal color palette library. Generate a vertical 3:4 editorial photograph of a tranquil rocky Mediterranean cove seen from above: pale limestone rocks framing clear jade and teal water, a little warm ivory sand, subtle deep blue shadows and small white sea foam. Refined analog travel photography, soft morning sun, tactile natural grain, striking simple composition with broad distinct areas of color to extract a five-color palette. No people, no boats, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. Single full-bleed photograph.
